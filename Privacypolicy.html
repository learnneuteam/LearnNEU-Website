Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

LearnNEU respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use LearnNEU, how it may be used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, LearnNEU may collect information such as:

  • Name or display name
  • Email address
  • Account and login information
  • Academic information voluntarily provided by the user
  • Study material uploaded by the user
  • Quiz responses and learning activity
  • Mistakes, revision records and performance reflections
  • Expected and actual examination results entered by the user

2. How We Use Information

Information collected through LearnNEU may be used to provide the platform's features and help users understand their learning patterns.

This may include generating learning summaries, maintaining mistake and revision records, displaying learning trends, and supporting reflection on academic performance.

3. Neuro Analysis

LearnNEU may present indicators related to selected learning and cognitive processes, such as focus or working memory.

These indicators are intended for educational reflection and are not medical, psychological, neurological, or clinical diagnoses.

LearnNEU does not claim to determine a user's intelligence, fixed learning capacity, or medical condition from these indicators.

4. Uploaded Content

Users should only upload content that they have the right to use. Users are responsible for the information and materials they choose to submit to LearnNEU.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with LearnNEU. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security of information transmitted or stored electronically.

6. Data Sharing

LearnNEU does not intend to sell users' personal information. Information may be processed or stored by technical service providers required to operate the platform.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or where necessary to protect the security and integrity of the service.

7. Student and Minor Users

LearnNEU may be used by students, including users who may be under the age of 18. Students should use the platform with the knowledge and, where required, supervision of a parent, guardian, school, or other responsible adult.

Users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive personal information to the platform.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

LearnNEU may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for authentication, preferences, security, or basic website functionality.

9. Your Choices

Users may request information about personal data associated with their account or request correction or deletion where applicable.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as LearnNEU develops. Changes will be reflected on this page together with an updated revision date.
