The idea

Learning is more than a score.

Marks tell you what happened. LearnNEU is designed to help students understand the learning patterns behind those results.

Instead of simply giving another score or another quiz, LearnNEU brings together learning behaviour, cognitive indicators, study material, mistakes, revision and examination performance.
